◈ Weather alerts
Know before it changes

Like the big weather apps, ATLAS sends you a notification when the weather where you are is about to change. Pick what you want to hear about.

WHAT AN ALERT LOOKS LIKE
ATLAS Weathernow
◈ Rain starting in 20 min

Light rain until about 16:00. Take a coat, Traveller.

ATLAS Weather2 min ago
⚠ Extreme weather approaching

Thunderstorm expected from 18:00. Gusts up to 70 km/h.

Alerts arrive even when ATLAS is closed. They use the place you last searched or located, rounded to about 11 km. Rain and temperature alerts stay quiet from 23:00 to 07:00. On iPhone, add ATLAS to your Home Screen first (Share ▸ Add to Home Screen). Turning alerts off deletes your sign-up. How your data is used
